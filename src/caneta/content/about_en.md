# About CANETA

**C.A.N.E.T.A.** — from the Portuguese *Central de Assistência e Navegação para Escrita de Textos Avançados* (Assistance and Navigation Hub for Advanced Text Writing). *Caneta* means "pen".

CANETA is an **open** scientific writing assistant. It takes you from draft to submission **one section at a time**, focused on Latin American **Diamond Open Access** journals: free to read and free to publish.

## What it is for

- **Writing with method.** Bring a finished paper or start from scratch. CANETA splits the work into sections; each one is reviewed independently by reviewers that quote the exact passage. **You decide** each finding before moving on.
- **Citing without inventing.** Citations come from open literature (SciELO, DOAJ, Redalyc, LA Referencia) found by CANETA, or from your own material. The AI never produces bibliographic data; invented citations are removed automatically.
- **Comparing results.** In results and discussion, CANETA downloads the full text of related work and checks quoted numbers against the original.
- **Choosing the journal.** It suggests indexed Diamond journals (Qualis 2021-2024, citations, scope) and uses the journal's own template.

## Why it exists

Latin America trains researchers who can change society, and pays two or three times for what they produce.

In 2026 Brazil's CAPES signed agreements worth about **R$ 1 billion (US$ 215 million) for 2026-2028** with Elsevier, Springer Nature and ACM, and on top of that collection came a proprietary AI tool, Elsevier's **LeapSpace**.

The alternative already exists and is ours: **SciELO** (since 1997-1998), **Redalyc**, **LA Referencia** and hundreds of university-run Diamond journals. CANETA is a public tool for writing within that path.

## The book and the manifesto

CANETA grows out of the open book ***Aprendizado de Mundo Aberto em Poços de Petróleo*** (Open-World Learning in Oil Wells), by authors from UFAL (LCCV) and Petrobras.

- **About the book:** ISBN 978-65-02-28836-8, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22306796](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22306796), CC BY 4.0.
- **How it was published:** in a [public repository](https://github.com/LucaswasTaken/Aprendizado-de-Mundo-Aberto-em-Petroleo).
- **Its manifesto:** *Livros de Mundo Aberto* argues that paying to publish and paying to read are the same fence.

The **CANETA Manifesto** below (in Portuguese) brings those ideas to paper writing, AI and Latin American sovereignty.

## Your data

- **Everything runs on your computer**, and manuscripts live in `Documents/CANETA`.
- **AI is optional.** Only the section text and the sources found are sent, and every use is logged under *Transparency*.
- **Optional local features:** OCR for scanned PDFs and meaning-based search with multilingual embeddings.
